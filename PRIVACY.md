# Privacy policy for Knight's Tour

Last updated: 10 November 2026

Knight's Tour is a puzzle game made by Nektarios. It is free, open source (MIT License) and has no ads.

## The short version

The game collects nothing and sends nothing. It never connects to the internet.

## What the game stores, and where

To remember your game between sessions, the game saves a few things **on your device only**:

- the high score lists, with the names typed in for them
- your options (sound, theme, board, helps)
- which puzzles are solved and your daily challenge results
- which "Did you know?" facts and tips you have already seen

This data never leaves your device through the game. The developer cannot see it. Uninstalling the game, or clearing its data, deletes it.

On Android, the system's own backup (if you have it turned on) may copy the game's data to your Google account, like it does for most apps. That backup belongs to you and is handled by Google, not by the game. You can turn it off in Android's settings.

## What the game does not do

- No accounts or sign-in
- No internet access, no analytics, no tracking, no ads
- No access to your contacts, location, camera, microphone or files
- No sharing of any data with anyone

## Children

The game is suitable for children. It collects no personal information from anyone, of any age.

## Changes

If this policy changes, the new version will be posted here with a new date.
